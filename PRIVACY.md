# Privacy policy

tmuxhints does not collect, store remotely, or transmit any personal data or usage data.
It makes no network connections.

Its settings (the hotkey and your tmux prefix) and your optional `cheatsheet.json` are
stored only on your computer, in the folder shown by `tmuxhints config path`, and are
removed when you delete that folder or uninstall the Microsoft Store version.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/cicorias/tmuxhints/issues.
